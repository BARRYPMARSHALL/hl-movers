Tap any coin name to open that market on Hyperliquid. Source: Hyperliquid's public info API — no server, no login, nothing stored.
Price, 24h, volume, open interest and funding are live — this page fetches them itself and refreshes every 60 seconds. 1h and 4h come from a snapshot built every 15 minutes over the tradeable markets (≥$5M daily volume and open interest): they need one candle request per market, and doing that in every visitor's browser rate-limits the exchange for everyone. Untick the filter to see all 178 markets — the thin ones then show "—" for 1h and 4h. 24h is the venue's own mark-vs-prev-day reference, so it matches what the exchange shows. Funding is the 8-hour rate annualised (×24×365); a single print is not carry — check the trend before acting on an outlier.
Illiquid markets are included because the table is meant to be complete: the median market turns over about $573k a day, so a large percentage on a thin name is a headline rather than a trade. Use the tradeable filter.
